Operational Guide

Policy Engagement Readout Pipeline

How to go from raw event notes → polished strategic readout

What this system does

After attending a policy event, you log it via a Google Form. Then — when you're ready — you add your detailed notes (paste them or link a Google Doc). The system auto-extracts structured intelligence using AI. When you want to compile a readout for leadership, you invoke the workflow and it produces a polished strategic document published to Google Docs, GitHub Pages, and Slack.

2

Add your detailed notes

Within a day or two of the event

Within 48 hours

This is where the real intelligence goes. You have two options — use whichever is easier:

Option A: Write in a Google Doc

Best when your notes are long or collaborative

  1. Write your detailed notes in a Google Doc (any format — raw is fine)
  2. Open Chain and say:
    Enrich "RUSI Sanctions" using this doc: [paste Google Doc URL]
  3. Chain invokes the workflow, which reads the doc and auto-extracts everything

Option B: Paste notes directly

Best when your notes are shorter

  1. Open Chain and say:
    Enrich "FINMA Bilateral" with these notes:
  2. Paste your raw notes after the prompt
  3. Chain invokes the workflow, AI extracts everything
What the AI auto-extracts from your raw notes
  • Competitors present — Elliptic, Crystal, TRM
  • Clients in the room — identifies Chainalysis customers
  • Key attendees — names, roles, organisations
  • Our contributions — what we argued, presented, pushed
  • Key insights — intelligence not publicly available
  • Doors opened — relationships, follow-ups created
  • Recommended actions — what to do next
  • Priority mapping — maps to P1/P2/P3 strategic priorities
  • Internal inform — which teams need to know (Product, Sales, Research, etc.)
📍 Where the enriched data lands
"Event Enrichments" tab in the tracker sheet — one row per event with all extracted fields. You can review and edit this if the AI got anything wrong.
💡
What makes good notes: Focus on what we said (our arguments, positions, data we presented), not just what we heard. Include direct quotes where you can. Name names. Flag anything that's not public.
↓
3

Generate the readout

When you're ready to compile for leadership

End of engagement period

Once all events are enriched, you generate the readout. Open Chain and say:

Generate a readout covering the last 3 weeks of events.
Title: "Engagement Readout"
Date range: "9–25 June 2026"
Authors: "Jordan Wain & Matthias B-L"
Publish to GitHub Pages.

Chain invokes the workflow in generate_readout mode. It pulls all enriched events from the tracker, generates the readout, and publishes it.

What the readout includes (automatically)
  • Hero header with title, authors, date range
  • Scorecard — events, bilaterals, intel items, outcomes, competitors, actions
  • Events attended — two-column grid (Week 1 / Week 2)
  • Executive summary — outcomes first → what we pushed → access vs competitors
  • Priority mapping — P1/P2/P3 buckets against strategic priorities
  • Strategic takeaways — with P-tags, source event pills, and "→ Inform" lines
  • Insights not available online — flagged non-public intel
  • Competitive positioning — multi-week table
  • Event detail — collapsible cards with full content
  • Action tracker — table with priority colours, owners, deadlines, status

Outputs produced:

📄 Google Doc — for sharing with boss
🌐 GitHub Pages — viewable online by anyone with the link
💬 Slack notification — posted automatically
📍 Where to find the outputs
Google Doc: Link returned by Chain and posted to Slack
GitHub Pages: jordan-wain.github.io/policy-dashboard/engagement-readout.html
Slack: Notification posts to the policy channel automatically

4

Refine in Chain (optional)

If the readout needs adjustments

Optional

The generated readout is a strong first draft. If you want to adjust it — add events, tighten messaging, change emphasis — work with Chain directly:

  • "Add a takeaway about tokenised deposits rising as a priority"
  • "The exec summary needs to lead with what we pushed, not what we heard"
  • "Fold in this new event from Wednesday evening"
  • "Tighten the messaging — my boss loses concentration fast"
  • "Publish the updated version to GitHub Pages"

Chain edits the HTML directly, re-publishes to GitHub Pages, and updates the Google Doc.

⚡ Shortcut: Quick Readout (skip enrichment)

If you're in a hurry and have all your notes ready, you can skip Steps 1–2 and go straight to readout. Open Chain and say:

Generate a quick readout from these notes and this Google Doc:
[paste your notes]
Google Docs: [URL 1], [URL 2]

This uses quick_readout mode — notes go straight to the AI without being saved to the enrichment tracker. Useful for one-off readouts but you lose the structured data in the sheet.
