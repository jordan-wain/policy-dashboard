Policy Engagement Readout

How it works — two steps

After each event, fill in the Google Form — including your detailed notes or a link to your Google Doc. When you want a readout, open Chain and say "generate my readout." That's it. The system reads your form responses, extracts intelligence using AI, and produces a polished strategic readout — published to Google Docs, GitHub Pages, and Slack.

1

Fill in the form

After each event — takes 5 minutes

Log the event and add your notes in one go. The key field is "Detailed Notes/Handover" — paste your raw notes directly, or paste a Google Doc link and the system reads it automatically.

2

Say "generate my readout"

When you're ready to compile — one sentence in Chain

Open Chain and tell it what you want. It reads all your form responses, auto-enriches them with AI, and produces the readout. Examples:

"Generate my readout for the last three weeks. Title: Engagement Readout. Date range: 9–25 June 2026."
"Generate a readout from my recent events. Publish to GitHub Pages."

The system automatically:

  1. Reads all your form responses from the tracker sheet
  2. Reads any linked Google Docs
  3. Uses AI to extract: competitors, contributions, insights, actions, priority mapping, internal team recommendations
  4. Generates the readout in the agreed format (hero header, scorecard, exec summary with outcomes first, priority mapping, takeaways with P-tags and inform lines, competitive table, action tracker)
  5. Publishes to Google Doc, GitHub Pages, and Slack

Want to refine it? Just keep talking to Chain: "tighten the exec summary", "add a takeaway about tokenised deposits", "my boss loses concentration fast — make it more scannable."
