FCA Consultation Impact Assessment

Chainalysis Policy Advocacy — UK Cryptoasset Regime

"The UK cryptoasset regime now requires the kind of analytics capabilities we provide — and we helped shape that requirement across 6 formal consultation responses over 12 months."

Final Policy Statements — 25 June 2026

The FCA published five Policy Statements setting out the final rules for the UK's cryptoasset regime. Rules take effect October 2027, with an authorisation gateway opening September 2027. Click any PS below for detail.

PS26/9
▶
Admissions, Disclosures & Market Abuse

Responds to CP25/41.

• Industry-led market abuse model — UK QCATPs take primary detection role, not FCA

• On-chain monitoring: large firms only (≥£10m), narrowed to platform-linked wallets

• Price dislocation detection required for ALL platforms

• Fungibility exception removed — each cryptoasset needs its own QCDD

• DTI standard specified; 5-year record retention (7 on request)

• 'Legitimate reasons' LMP removed; coin burning and stabilisation kept

Our influence: 31% (8/26 adopted or partial)

PS26/10
▶
Stablecoin Issuance

Responds to CP25/14.

• Statutory trust for backing assets confirmed

• Simplified backing composition — 5% excess allowed

• Intragroup custody up to 20%

• Redemption: max 5 business days; 30 for large with criteria

• Consumer Duty applies to stablecoin issuance

• UK QCATPs cannot refuse stablecoin admission on QCDD quality

Our influence: 19% (5/26)

PS26/11
▶
Regulated Activities

Responds to CP25/40-42 and CP26/04.

• DeFi: case-by-case; decentralisation indicators in guidance later 2026

• Best execution: overarching arrangements, principles-based

• Custody: CASS 17 applied; float model 2%

• Lending/borrowing framework with borrower protections

• Staking: regulated activity with disclosure requirements

Our influence: 31% (4/13)

PS26/12
▶
Prudential

Capital and prudential elements of CP25/40-42.

• K-SII reduced 2% → 1%; two-tier classification ditched

• Single 40% net risk position for K-NCP

• UK stablecoin orders exempted from cryptoasset order definition

Outside our core advocacy focus

PS26/13
▶
FCA Handbook

Responds to CP25/25, CP26/04, CP26/13.

• Consumer Duty applied to all regulated crypto activities

• SM&CR and SYSC applied with crypto-specific risk expectations

• Financial crime: blockchain analytics capabilities "expected"

• FCA will not mandate specific technology but expects appropriate tools

• Perimeter guidance: further PS before Sep 2027 gateway

Our influence: 17% (11/63)

📈 What This Means Commercially

On-chain monitoring required
Large UK QCATPs must have on-chain monitoring capabilities for market abuse detection. PS26/9 §3.52–3.57.
Blockchain analytics "expected"
PS26/13 states firms should apply "crypto-specific risks including blockchain analytics capabilities" for financial crime controls.
325 firms in scope
FCA updated market participant estimate from 180 to 325 firms — all needing compliant systems by Oct 2027.
